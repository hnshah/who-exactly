# Shared result-quality standard

The standard has three parts: **Evidence, Judgment, Honesty**.

### Evidence — Can the user check important claims?

Individual dated sources for person-level claims. Clear original author. No fabricated quotes. Distinguish an author's self-report from our direct observation of product use. If unavailable, don't pad the output.

### Judgment — Did we choose what matters for this job?

Relevant actor, task, trigger, setup, actual workaround and near-term decision. Successful incumbent methods count as evidence. Distinguish plausible people from genuine job-qualified candidates. Reject superficial matches. Prefer an answer with two strong cases and a concrete question over a list of ten weak profiles.

### Honesty — Does the output show what it couldn't establish?

Label missing setup, incomplete X search coverage, unavailable thread context, unsupported personal X scope, source age and uncertainty. Do not treat the absence of a public post as an absence of practitioners. Do not infer opt-in or repeated value.

## Preferred output order

Research question and job definition → actual sources/search mode → qualifying cases → non-fits and counterexamples → uncertainty → concrete next research step. Draft invitations only when the user asks or when they are the current task. Avoid dumping raw search results and unnecessary tables when a concise conclusion is sufficient.

## Outcome measures (not claimed yet)

Source-verified qualifying firsthand episodes per bounded mission; wrong-person rate; counterfeit/unresolvable citation rate; correctly disclosed no-results cases; quality of invitation; opted-in interviews and directly observed product results tracked separately. Measured results require a stated denominator, a reviewed sample and a date.
