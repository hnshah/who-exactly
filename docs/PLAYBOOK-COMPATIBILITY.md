# Early Access Playbook compatibility

Canonical educational resource: **The Early Access Playbook** by Hiten Shah ([public Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bo9n52j_M4Nx094TObZQVAq1htlB6h9XlySLgFsH710/edit)). It is a separate resource with its own versions; Who Exactly? does not replace it.

Who Exactly? was designed against the v1.1 play names. As of 10-27-2026 the public document is titled v2.0 and the plays were renamed:

| # | v1.1 name | v2.0 name (public doc, 10-27-2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Define the promise | Start with something worth changing |
| 02 | Assemble the group | Find the people doing the work |
| 03 | Find the Pattern of Pain | Find where the old way fails |
| 04 | Watch real work | Put the product in the job |
| 05 | Improve and retest | Decide what to change |
| 06 | Prove value and decide | Find out what they choose next time |

Who Exactly? is **a specialist supporting part of Play 02**. It accepts an existing promise or working bet when available, but does not require users to finish a form before researching. It can produce candidate sources and research-ready questions for Play 03. It cannot claim to perform Play 04 just because someone posted about their workflow.

Who Exactly? is not the Early Access Bot. A fuller Early Access Bot covering the whole Playbook is a possible future, separate product.

## Stable handoff contract

**Input:** product context; decision; actor; job trigger; current action and tool; eligibility; explicit exclusion signals; actual available X sources.

**Output:** Job Card; retrieved post episode records; candidates classified included/possible/rejected/unknown; source provenance; invitations drafted/reviewed; separately recorded opt-ins; next research decision.

**Versioning:** keep these inputs and outputs stable even if Playbook sections are renamed or re-ordered (as happened between v1.1 and v2.0). Point the Playbook at the Bot only when the working Bot is tested and its availability is accurately described.

## Historical and private evidence

Real research behind the Beam example (described publicly in the Playbook) is validation context, **not** a public audience dataset or proof of conversion. No participant records are bundled in this repository, and none may be used as a fixture.
