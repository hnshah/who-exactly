# Job evidence map

## Mission

Turn a product description or assumption into the **specific job episode** worth investigating, with eligibility criteria a skeptic can apply to positive and negative results.

## Use this skill when

- The founder asks who their earliest users should be, but has only a persona or feature concept.
- The target market is broad, and actual behavior has not been specified.
- A search requires a bounded definition of relevant work.

## Do not use this skill when

- A Job Card exists and the task is locating actual public X posts: use `x-people-safari`.
- The objective is comparing existing methods and tools: use `workaround-atlas`.
- The objective is contacting specific qualified people: use `cohort-assembly`.

## Input and output

Input: product/feature or desired result; actor and workflow when known. Output: Job Card, qualifying and disqualifying evidence, research-channel assessment and next check. Follow `assets/JOB-CARD.md`.

## Method

1. Identify the actor, recent triggering event, actual action, environment, current alternative and intended outcome. A company type, job title or interest isn't the job.
2. Mark facts, inferred hypotheses and genuinely missing information separately. Read `references/EVIDENCE.md` and `references/CONTEXT.md`.
3. Write at least three observable positive signals. Prefer dated first-person task episodes over excitement about the proposed product.
4. Write at least two important non-fits: another actual job, incompatible environment or an existing method that works adequately.
5. Generate exact X search ideas from the verbs, artifacts, triggers and workaround terms people use, not marketing jargon.
6. Decide whether public X is a plausible research channel. If essential work is confidential or rarely described, propose permitted alternative research channels; don't claim X coverage.
7. Supply one candidate qualification question and the type of contrary evidence that could change the founder's decision.

## Quality and stopping rules

Read `references/QUALITY.md` and `references/ACCESS.md`. Do not invent people, real-world source links or customer prevalence. No bio or follower count can establish real work. A Job Card is a **research hypothesis**, not evidence of demand.

## Handoff

To actually search, pass the Job Card to `x-people-safari`. To analyze existing alternatives, use `workaround-atlas`. Do not invoke every neighboring skill without a reason.
