# Cohort assembly

## Mission

Help founders move from **sourced possible practitioners** to an opt-in learning relationship, without treating discovery, willingness and actual product use as equivalent.

## Use this skill when

- The founder wants to invite a particular source-reviewed person to an interview or early-access test.
- They need a minimal research group, participant expectations or a way to track opt-in.
- They want to challenge the next cohort with a different setup or less founder advantage.

## Do not use this skill when

- No credible people have been identified yet: use `x-people-safari`.
- The question is who qualifies for a job: use `job-evidence-map`.
- The question is about alternatives and pain patterns: use `workaround-atlas`.

## Required input/output

Input: specific sourced candidate review, actual readiness (idea / prototype / usable product), research aim, genuine available participation method. Output **individualized reviewed drafts**, a Cohort Plan and optional Access Contract; no messages sent by default. See `assets/INVITATION.md` and `assets/COHORT-PLAN.md`.

## Method

1. Reopen the candidate evidence and ensure the quoted episode is the candidate's own claim; if context is uncertain, explicitly say so rather than personalizing incorrectly.
2. Decide what participation is actually being requested: answer a question about the last occurrence, join a short interview, test a prototype, or attempt a specific real task. Match language to product readiness.
3. Draft a concise, context-specific reply or DM referencing only genuine relevant context, the ask, approximate time/effort if known, and an easy way to decline. Do not present a public post as permission to contact them.
4. If the user requests account-specific X context to improve the draft, check actual plugin read capabilities, and request an X connection only if needed. Follow `references/ACCESS.md`.
5. Treat sending as a **separate** action. Check whether the runtime actually supports the relevant X write operation and require explicit approval of recipient, account and exact message. Otherwise leave copyable drafts.
6. Track stages individually: discovered, eligible, invited, opted_in, enabled, attempted_real_job, observed_first_value, repeat_attempted. Never count all as conversions. If no contact occurred, leave invitation status `draft`.
7. Write an Access Contract: who/which job, what can actually be tried, what participants receive, expected involvement, permissions, feedback method, owner, departure option and expansion criteria.
8. Propose a later group sharing the core job but differing meaningfully in workflow, constraints, geography or founder relationship. Never expose one participant's private notes to a different group.

## Quality bar

Read `references/EVIDENCE.md`, `references/QUALITY.md` and `references/CONTEXT.md`. The founder decides whether to invite. Real agreement is required before anyone is recorded as opted in. Participation alone is not product value; move to the Playbook's observation and retesting plays for that.

## Handoff

Source discovery belongs to `x-people-safari`; product observation belongs to Play 04 of the Early Access Playbook, not to this skill.
