# Prefer evidence of completed work over profile affinity

A profile mentioning a product, profession, or hobby is an insufficient basis
for concluding the person recently performed the job being studied.

**Proposed method:** search for firsthand episodes, identify the author and
specific task, and explicitly preserve non-fit examples. Verify the source
rather than assuming that a match to keywords proves the job.

This is a methodological draft extracted from our current research procedures,
not a new empirical finding about any user or a retrieved X source.
